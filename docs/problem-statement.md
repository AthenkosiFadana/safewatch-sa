# Problem Statement — SafeWatch SA

## Context

South Africa is a particularly relevant environment for a community safety platform. SAPS
reported **664,758 contact-crime cases in 2024/25**, and its crime-statistics system is intended
to support planning and policy through better crime data.

Residents, neighbourhood watches and community leaders often lack a shared, near-real-time view
of what is happening around them. Information spreads informally (WhatsApp groups, social posts)
with no structure, no deduplication and no analytics.

## The gap

1. **Visibility** — residents cannot see local incident patterns easily.
2. **Structure** — informal reports have no category, severity, location or status.
3. **Alerts** — no automatic notification when incidents spike in a specific area.
4. **Trust** — unverified claims spread without any moderation or status workflow.

## Solution

SafeWatch SA is a cloud-based community safety monitoring platform that lets communities report,
visualise and monitor safety incidents while providing timely community alerts and data-driven
insights.

### Tagline

> **See it. Report it. Stay informed.**

## Scope and boundaries

SafeWatch SA **does not**:

- replace SAPS or act as an alternative emergency service,
- determine guilt or accuse individuals,
- present community reports as official crime statistics.

SafeWatch SA **does**:

- collect and structure community safety reports,
- visualise them on a map with severity colours,
- raise area-based safety alerts,
- provide analytics over clearly-labelled data sources,
- moderate reports through an explicit status workflow.

### Emergency guidance

SAPS directs the public to **10111** for emergencies and to police stations for formal crime
reporting. SafeWatch SA surfaces this guidance throughout the interface (footer, report form,
documentation) and is explicitly framed as a community information platform.

## Data source strategy

| Source | Tag | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community reports | `COMMUNITY` | Generated by SafeWatch users |
| Official statistics | `SAPS_OFFICIAL` | Published SAPS statistics where legally/technically appropriate |
| Demo data | `DEMO` | Synthetic data for development and portfolio demonstration |

We do not scrape social media crime posts and present them as official statistics.

## Success criteria (MVP v1.0)

- A resident can register, log in and submit a validated incident report.
- Incidents appear on an interactive map coloured by severity.
- Three or more incidents in one area within two hours raise an automatic safety alert.
- An admin can move a report through PENDING → UNDER REVIEW → VERIFIED → RESOLVED or REJECT it.
- Analytics show category split, time-of-day pattern, hotspots and a 7-day trend.
- The system runs locally with no cloud credentials and deploys to AWS with SAM.
